# numpy is pinned on Intel Macs: there torch resolves to 2.2.2 (the last release with x86_64 Mac
# wheels) and that build is compiled against the numpy 1.x ABI. Under numpy 2.x torch imports with
# "Failed to initialize NumPy: _ARRAY_API not found" and every tensor->numpy conversion then raises
# "Numpy is not available", so every /motion answers 500. torch 2.2.2 declares no numpy bound of its
# own, which is why the pin has to live here. Apple Silicon and Linux get a modern torch and stay on
# numpy 2.x. scipy needs no pin of its own: under numpy<2 the resolver backtracks it to the 1.17.x
# line, the last one that still accepts numpy 1.26.
numpy<2; sys_platform == "darwin" and platform_machine == "x86_64"
numpy; sys_platform != "darwin" or platform_machine != "x86_64"
scipy
fastapi
